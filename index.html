How to use this reader

Use Heleios to support your own translation and close reading. Open a passage, read the Greek, and select words to explore their meanings and grammatical relationships.

  • Select a Greek word to explore its lemma, morphology, and dictionary entry.
  • Follow the sentence diagram to see subjects, verbs, objects, and links between related words, including across verse lines.
  • Consult the searchable Smyth grammar. Text-specific resources are also included within the readers, where available.

About Heleios

Grammatical annotations can contain gaps or errors, and dictionary excerpts may need the context of the full entry. Use these aids alongside your own reading and judgment.

Heleios owes a particular debt to the Perseus Digital Library and to the scholars and projects whose texts and reference works make this reader possible. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with the Perseus Digital Library.
